# Evidence grading for TellTag V7

**Direct study:** an indicator was experimentally investigated on the named food, but a different formulation/package than TellTag. **Related food family:** demonstrated for generic fish or poultry, not necessarily Atlantic salmon or the packaging chosen here. **Not established:** literature in this catalog does not support that specific pairing. None of these categories is evidence of regulatory food-contact suitability.

## Salmon

- Salmon has species- and packaging-dependent spoilage pathways. A 2025 study identified methylglyoxal, benzyl alcohol and acetic acid as candidate volatile markers, based on targeted metabolomics and machine learning. These are *not* selectively measured by simple base-sensitive pH dyes (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41267257/).
- Salmon spoilage can release trimethylamine and other amines. Indicator systems targeting volatile bases are scientifically relevant, but the particular dye will not necessarily detect *early* degradation or distinguish it from other alkaline gases (https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/87559129.2023.2221332).
- A salmon-specific amine-responsive colorimetric/fluorescent probe was reported in 2025; V7 lists it as an experimental research option without pretending to model its reaction or certify food contact (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0003267025009195).
- The published salmon TVB-N endpoints in the project are **two points only** (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5756197/).

## Chicken

- A BCP indicator was tested with chicken breast by contact (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6049484/).
- A BCP/methyl-red dual-indicator was studied for chicken fillets under a specific coating/storing protocol (https://www.mdpi.com/2304-8158/11/21/3533).
- The project’s 2026 vacuum-packed chicken study shows that TVB-N and food tissue pH are not simple monotonic substitutes for all spoilage markers (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13298445/).

## Beef

- The project includes six points of beef bacterial-count and TVB-N measurements in a 4°C research study, not a domestic storage chart (https://www.mdpi.com/2304-8158/14/23/4017).

## Prices

- The user’s Google Drive research assessed commercially available temperature indicators, a 2016 production-cost estimate, and historical Canadian retail prices. These are contextual benchmarks, *not current quotes for TellTag* (https://docs.google.com/document/d/1v-j2m8XzQJ_ZYsamB4C42fHiN5FoeD3-knU2Pbxo2c4/edit).
- V7 default prices for membrane film, dye, coating binders, adhesives, assembly, and QA were **invented solely as visible editable demonstration assumptions**. A real BOM must use supplier-provided grade-specific cost, converting from actual order sizes and wastage.
